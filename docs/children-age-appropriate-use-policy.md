# UNLXCK Children & Age-Appropriate Use Policy

## Scope
UNLXCK may be used by athletes aged **13+**. Users under 18 are children for this policy and must receive age-appropriate privacy and safety protections.

## Age Rules
- **Under 13:** not supported at initial public launch.
- **13–15:** early-teen protections and simpler explanations.
- **16–17:** high-privacy child protections with more mature wording.
- **18+:** standard adult flow.

Age assurance must be proportionate to risk and collect only what is needed to establish the user’s age band.

## Privacy Defaults
For under-18s:
- use high-privacy settings by default;
- collect only data needed for the active feature;
- do not make child data visible to other users or third parties by default;
- do not use nudges to obtain unnecessary data or weaken privacy;
- give clear, age-appropriate explanations when data use changes;
- provide easy tools to exercise privacy rights or report concerns.

## Profiling & Personalisation
Training personalisation that is necessary to provide the requested core service may operate only with appropriate safeguards and clear explanation. Any non-essential profiling must default off unless a documented best-interests reason justifies otherwise.

## Health Data & Consent
UNLXCK must explain health-data processing in language the child can understand. Where UNLXCK relies on consent, users aged 13+ may generally provide their own consent for an online service under UK data-protection law, subject to valid-consent and capacity requirements. Health-data consent must remain explicit, separate and withdrawable.

Do not assume that parental access or consent is automatically required for every 13–17-year-old. Parental/trusted-adult involvement should be encouraged where risk or understanding warrants it and handled transparently to the child.

## Safety Rules for Under-18s
- Do not provide aggressive weight-cut, dehydration or automated water-cut protocols.
- Do not present injury/readiness outputs as diagnosis or treatment.
- Escalate red-flag symptoms to appropriate professional support.
- Do not use XP, streaks, notifications or other nudges to encourage a child to train through pain, ignore a stop/restriction decision, disclose more health data, or weaken privacy settings.

## Coach / Parent / Gym Access
Before any coach, parent or gym account can view a junior athlete’s data, UNLXCK must define:
- exactly what data is visible;
- the lawful basis and safeguarding purpose;
- whether the child can see that access;
- how access is granted, changed and revoked; and
- which data remains private to the athlete.

No broad junior-athlete data sharing is permitted by default.

## Product Requirements Before Public Launch
1. Collect or establish age band during signup/onboarding.
2. Apply child-appropriate privacy defaults automatically.
3. Provide child-readable Privacy Notice and consent wording.
4. Record Terms acceptance and health-data consent separately and by version/time.
5. Update the DPIA for child users, weight-cut safeguards, profiling and coach/parent sharing.
6. Test that gamification and notifications cannot undermine safety or privacy controls.

## Review Trigger
Reassess this policy before enabling under-13 access, social/community features, location sharing, coach/parent visibility, advertising, new profiling, or materially different health/AI functionality.

This is an internal compliance policy and does not replace legal advice.
