# UNLXCK Data Breach & User Rights Procedure

**Internal procedure**

## Privacy contact
Requests and privacy complaints should be sent to: **support@unlxck.com**.

## 1. User data requests
This covers access, deletion, correction, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of health-data consent.

### Process
1. Record the request and date received.
2. Verify identity only where reasonably necessary.
3. Identify relevant UNLXCK systems and processors.
4. Apply any required restriction while the request is assessed.
5. Complete the request without undue delay and within the applicable legal deadline.
6. Record the outcome, including any lawful reason data was retained or a request was refused.

Subject access requests should normally be completed within one month. Where a lawful extension applies, notify the requester within the initial period.

Withdrawal of health-data consent stops future processing that relies on that consent. It does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. Health-dependent product features must degrade safely after withdrawal.

## 2. Personal data breaches
A suspected breach must be handled immediately.

### Contain
- stop or limit the exposure;
- revoke compromised access, sessions, tokens or credentials;
- isolate affected systems where necessary; and
- preserve evidence needed to investigate.

### Assess
Record:
- what happened and when;
- data and systems affected;
- number/type of users affected;
- whether health data or children's data is involved;
- whether credentials or administrator/service-role access is affected;
- likely consequences; and
- actions already taken.

### Notify
Assess the likely risk to people's rights and freedoms.

Where notification to the ICO is required, report without undue delay and, where feasible, within **72 hours of becoming aware** of the breach. Do not delay an initial report solely because every detail is not yet known.

Where the breach is likely to result in a **high risk** to affected people, assess whether they must also be informed without undue delay.

### Record
Record every personal-data breach, including breaches not reported to the ICO, together with the risk assessment and reason for the reporting decision.

## 3. UNLXCK urgent-escalation events
Treat the following as requiring immediate privacy/security assessment:

- injury, health or weight data exposed to an unauthorised person;
- children's personal data exposed;
- one athlete can access another athlete's data;
- Supabase RLS or other access-control failure;
- administrator or service-role credentials exposed;
- health data sent to an unintended third party;
- account credentials or authentication tokens compromised.

## 4. Ownership
**Privacy owner:** [NAME / ROLE]

The privacy owner is responsible for tracking deadlines, coordinating technical investigation, deciding whether specialist/legal advice is required and documenting the final outcome.

## 5. Review
Review this procedure after any material breach, major processor/security change or significant change to UK data-protection requirements.
