# UNLXCK Intended Purpose & Regulatory Boundary

## Intended purpose

UNLXCK is a combat-sport training and performance application for athletes. It uses athlete-provided training, recovery, schedule, nutrition and injury information to organise fight-camp training, adjust training load, identify when training should be reduced or stopped, and direct athletes to seek appropriate professional assessment where relevant.

UNLXCK is intended to support **training, performance, recovery awareness and risk-aware exercise selection**. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a disease or injury, provide a medical diagnosis or prognosis, replace a doctor/physiotherapist or other qualified healthcare professional, or determine that an athlete is medically fit to train, spar or compete.

## Injury boundary

UNLXCK may:

- collect athlete-reported injury, pain and recovery information;
- restrict, remove or substitute training that could aggravate a reported problem;
- reduce or stop app-prescribed training when predefined safety rules are triggered;
- show conservative recovery-oriented exercise options; and
- advise the athlete to obtain professional medical assessment.

UNLXCK must **not** present these functions as diagnosing an injury, treating or rehabilitating a diagnosed condition, predicting clinical outcomes, clearing an athlete for participation, or replacing individual medical care.

Internal labels such as `medical_hold`, `restricted_rehab_only`, injury severity classifications and red-flag rules are safety-control states. User-facing wording must not imply a clinical diagnosis or medical decision.

## Nutrition and weight-cut boundary

UNLXCK may provide general sports-nutrition and weight-management guidance and flag potentially unsafe weight-cut conditions. It must not diagnose nutritional or medical conditions, prescribe treatment, or present weight-cut guidance as medically supervised care. High-risk situations must defer to an appropriately qualified professional.

## AI boundary

AI-generated content is subordinate to UNLXCK's deterministic safety rules. AI must not override injury restrictions, safety holds, red-flag rules or other server-owned safeguards. Athlete-facing copy must not imply that AI has clinically assessed the athlete.

## Claims and marketing rule

Product pages, app copy, social media, onboarding and promotional material must remain consistent with this intended purpose. Avoid claims that UNLXCK **diagnoses, treats, rehabilitates, medically monitors, predicts injury, clears athletes, or provides medical care** unless the product is deliberately taken through the applicable medical-device regulatory pathway.

## Change-control trigger

Reassess the regulatory position before releasing any feature or claim that:

- identifies or predicts a specific disease or injury;
- recommends treatment for a medical condition;
- measures or claims clinical recovery;
- provides return-to-play, return-to-sparring or medical-clearance decisions; or
- materially influences clinical diagnosis or treatment.

## Status

This document defines UNLXCK's current intended product boundary. It is an internal compliance control, not a substitute for formal legal or MHRA advice. Review it whenever injury, health, nutrition, AI or marketing functionality materially changes.
