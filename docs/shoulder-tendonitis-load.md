## Exact presentation and evidence

Only an active, owned left/right `shoulder_tendonitis` episode, mild severity,
with an explicit athlete report that their clinician described **non-traumatic
rotator cuff tendinopathy without a tear** can consider this option. The single
occasional selection lives in the existing injury editor, defaults to Not sure,
and is not a diagnosis or independent medical verification. Generic tendonitis,
outer shoulder pain, Better/Same responses and contact clearance cannot establish
this presentation. No specific cuff tendon, measured ROM, strength or load
capacity is inferred. Unknown presentation retains baseline; explicit conflicting
presentation receives existing recovery monitoring/medical routing.

Sources directly inspected on 18 October 2026:

- [2025 CPG, full text](https://www.orthopt.org/uploads/content_files/files/Rotator_Cuff_CPG.pdf),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2025.13182): recommendation 25 (Grade A)
  supports active rehabilitation including motor control and/or resistance
  exercises with various loads as initial treatment. History, examination,
  active/passive ROM, strength and differential diagnosis inform clinical care.
  The dosage discussion reports uncertainty over optimal load/dose and no
  established high-load superiority. This CPG covers a broader population,
  including partial-thickness tears; this pilot deliberately excludes tears.
  It does not validate a universal app LOAD gate, the selected wall variant or
  the app's conservative ceilings.
- [RJAH NHS rotator-cuff-related shoulder pain](https://www.rjah.nhs.uk/our-services/therapy/supported-self-care/rotator-cuff-related-shoulder-pain/),
  review date February 2026: wall push-ups, hands slightly wider than shoulders,
  slightly turned out, elbows below shoulders, body tall; three sets of eight.
  Moving feet further away increases demand. Guidance requires review after
  trauma/power loss, redness/swelling, systemic symptoms or persistent symptoms.
  Its symptom advice allows a small transient increase settling within 30 minutes
  and no worsening next day. This pilot uses the more conservative pain-free
  stop instruction and does not ask a new 30-minute question.

## Prescription and why LOAD

**Controlled wall push-ups**, `shoulder_tendonitis_wall_push_up`: **1 × 8,
maximum once daily**, stable wall, hands at shoulder height and slightly wider than shoulders,
slightly turned out, elbows below shoulders, body tall, feet fixed near wall,
slow small pain-free elbow bend and push back. No added resistance, hands lower,
feet further back, fatigue, automatic increase, overhead or contact work.

This introduces closed-chain bodyweight strengthening and cuff/shoulder-girdle
control beyond RESTORE's small unloaded external rotation. It does not isolate a
tendon or certify pressing capacity. Moderate is a conservative categorical
upper-limb weight-bearing demand, not a force measurement. One set, daily ceiling,
near-wall position and mild-only applicability are product limits, not a tested
complete treatment programme or validated clinical thresholds.

## Eligibility, reversals and restrictions

The shared engine requires exact current episode/side, current versioned
rehabilitation loading or sport-specific permission, actual reviewed RESTORE
performed as shown, attributable non-worsening during and next-day responses,
complete history and no unresolved setback. No fixed session count, elapsed-time
rule, measured dose for self-paced RESTORE or numeric pain score is invented.
Modified/skipped work does not stand in for prescribed completion.

Suspected/known tear or rupture, acute trauma/loss of function, dislocation,
recurrent instability, neurological symptoms, unexplained weakness, structural
concerns, frozen shoulder, biceps/labral/AC/cervical diagnoses and surgery are
excluded or held. Current medical/training restrictions remain authoritative.
Stop for pain, worsening during/after work, weakness or instability. New loss of
function or neurological symptoms requires assessment, not another self-test.

Because both arms and standing support are used, any other active injury holds
this new option; its permission cannot be borrowed. This intentionally restrictive
rule applies equally in Today, generation and unstarted saved-work validation.
Stable wall availability is explicit in existing equipment settings; presets do
not assume it. Started/completed snapshots and exposures remain historical.
DYNAMIC/RETURN remain closed, including with sport-specific permission.
