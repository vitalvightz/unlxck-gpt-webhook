Baseline: `3dc625d8` on `Main`, pulled before editing. Audit date: 16 October 2026. Draft PR: [#2904](https://github.com/vitalvightz/unlxck-gpt-webhook/pull/2904).

The confirmed failure crosses selection, composition, effective-dose authority and coverage windows. This PR corrects those contracts in their existing owners. It does not establish that every programme is optimal, that the full repository suite is green, or that the architecture is globally simpler.

## Architectural simplification: evidence and limits

Net added lines are not evidence of simplification. Production code still grows. The demonstrated consolidation is local:

- `_select_bounded_records` shrinks from **133 to 113 source lines**, including comments. The redundant second primary reservation is removed; selected trunk/strength retention occurs before family filling inside the same cap.
- Recomposition and restoration no longer carry different no-regression predicates. `_coverage_regresses` compares exact missing windows for recomposition, capacity trades and final acceptance. Gaining one week cannot conceal losing another. Evidence is collected once per verdict.
- The gas-tank-only secondary exception is replaced by the existing shared weakness maps.
- Effective-dose rejection no longer falls through to base-dose membership. Closed countdown rendering reads the shared render authority instead of a separate generic-body path.
- Model wording and bank notes remain subordinate to resolved current work and already authorised future work. No new progression templates or automatic load increases were introduced.

The capacity trade is **additional logic**, not simplification. It is retained because restoring the missing D-14 appointment otherwise raises the representative week above its requested five sessions. Inside the existing bounded repair transaction, one of at least two app-owned conditioning appointments may fund a priced candidate only while conditioning and all other selected-goal coverage survive. Coach roles, finished tails, compression and shared legality remain protected. The same realised-load governor verifies the trial.

No runtime module, planner manager, placement engine, collision doctrine or metadata system was added. This is corrected authority plus local consolidation, not a claim of completed global architecture reduction. Further deletion needs evidence of a redundant writer or unreachable branch, rather than a line-count target.

## Scientific justification

- **Repeated maintenance exposure:** reduced frequency/volume can preserve strength when relative intensity is retained, but precise athlete-specific minima are not established. This supports continuity and actual-dose checks, not a universal MMA prescription. Weekly eligible-window checking is a coaching policy with readiness, injury, cut and competition deferral. [Spiering et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33629972/).
- **Actual strength versus power:** combat-sport strength interventions improve relevant fitness qualities, but the reviewed Olympic combat sports and heterogeneous programmes do not establish one 27-day MMA protocol. Genuine heavy/ballistic hybrids can overlap; a power-only dose does not automatically maintain loaded strength. [Combat-sport systematic review, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36834211/).
- **Concurrent training:** the 43-study meta-analysis supports contextual programming; maximal strength and hypertrophy were generally compatible with concurrent aerobic work, while explosive-strength interference was more apparent within the same session. It does not establish an exact sparring-to-lifting interval. [Schumann et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34757594/).
- **Taper:** volume reduction can retain useful stimulus while reducing fatigue. Strength-sport evidence remains limited and cannot dictate exact MMA cutoffs. This change repairs eligible work earlier and preserves the existing fight-week owner, rather than copying powerlifting doses into MMA. [Bosquet et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17762369/), [Travis et al., 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7552788/).
- **Dose-dependent fatigue:** failure/non-failure evidence supports considering actual fatigue and proximity to failure rather than treating all meaningful work identically. No new physiological threshold was inferred. [Acute-fatigue meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34881412/).

The existing qualifying-dose floor is a planner minimum, not proof of optimal whole-body maintenance or strength development. Athlete history, familiar loading, proficiency and response data are needed for stronger individual claims. Exact D-day, fatigue and cut thresholds remain coaching policies; these papers do not validate each number.

## Verification and remaining limitations

- **528 passed:** goal repair/windows, effective dosing, pre-hard support, phase boundaries, consecutive recovery, late windows, composition, architecture closure and placement ownership.
- **243 passed:** 16 full intake-to-markdown/card scenarios, extra selection seeds, repeated seeded generation after another athlete, recorded production-response replay, source-dose/card fidelity, validator and execution-instruction checks.
- **4 passed:** model progression retains supported numerical and qualitative guidance, rejects an unsupported load increase or extra rest reduction, and preserves current working quantities.
- Ruff, import compilation and whitespace checks passed. These totals overlap earlier runs and are not a sum of unique tests.
- Goal/weakness delivery passed 96 tests in isolation earlier. A combined run returned 630 passed and one failing non-limiting-blister membership comparison; that comparison then passed alone. This order-dependent result remains unresolved and is not a clean full-suite result.
- Historical metadata checks returned 336 passed, 14 failed and 13 errors. Frozen inventory expects 2,855 records; historical replay also rejects existing XP-source drift. Some source-freeze assertions conflict with intentional planner edits. Expected snapshots were not rewritten to hide failures.
- Older coaching/progression suites still expect deterministic recovery to manufacture Progress/Easier instructions. Current Main delegates these to the final-session model; representative failures were reproduced on untouched Main. Their automatic-increment expectations are separate from this PR's numerical-advice safety check. Web rendering tests requiring `web/node_modules` were skipped; no frontend source changed.
- The **complete scenario validator is not all green**. A rep-based Sprawl-Burpee Complex lacks measured active seconds; `_conditioning_selected_active_work_seconds` accounts it as zero against a seconds target. This is a unit/accounting limitation, not proof of zero training stimulus. No seconds were invented from repetitions to pass validation.
- Frequency remains a soft target. Compatible work can exceed it with a warning, including existing tail cadences. The representative repair funds its own appointment, not every historical overage. Annotation, phase-pool and direct-countdown structural warnings also remain in the recorded artifact codes.
- The harness exercises actual deterministic plans, finalizer packets and cards. Recorded-response finalizer replay is tested; the synthetic matrix makes no live provider call and cannot establish live-model consistency or athlete outcomes. No production database was read/changed, dependency added or deployment made.
