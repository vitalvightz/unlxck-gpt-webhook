Before you come

Good to know

What's the age limit?

All our nights are 18+. Bring a valid photo ID, even if you look older. No ID, no entry.

Is there a dress code?

Dress to go out. Some venues set their own rules, which we'll list on the night's ticket page.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you can transfer yours to a friend up to 24 hours before doors.

What time should I arrive?

Come early for the shortest queue. Last entry is usually an hour before close.

Do you do private hire or venue partnerships?

Yes. Get in touch through the contact details at the bottom of the page.
