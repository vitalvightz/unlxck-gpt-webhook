# Weekly Schedule

## Week 1 — GPP (D-26 → D-20)

Focus: Use the available base window to restore structure and rebuild repeatability before chasing extra specificity..

### D-26 (Saturday) — Fight Visualisation
Why: Rehearse the phases this fight is most likely to be decided in.
- Tactical Picture: 6 minutes, optional mental rehearsal. No physical load.
  Step 1: Pick the phase you expect to spend most of the fight in: range, clinch or ground.
  Step 2: See your entry into that phase and the first thing you do once you arrive.
  Step 3: See how you get back out if it is not working.
  Step 4: Run it again with the opponent starting the phase instead of you.
  Cue: Know where you want the fight.

### D-26 (Saturday) — Cage Circle and Cut-Off
Why: Use light cage-orientation steps. Keep the stance and hips oriented to the opponent while controlling the escape lane; do not chase into open space.
- Cage Circle and Cut-Off: 2 x 90 sec technical sets, 60 sec reset
  Cue: Track a self-planned path, light partner circle, or random left/right visual cue. Close the escape lane while keeping hips and stance oriented.
  Cue Method: Self-administer a random visual cue (reaction-cue app or a randomized left/right/shot call sequence) so the read stays genuinely reactive without a helper.
  Side / Stance: Start in your orthodox stance and work both directions evenly.

### D-26 (Saturday) — Strength
- Kettlebell Swing — 3x4 reps at crisp intent; rest 90s.
  Cue: Explosive hip-hinge option for repeated alactic power; end the set when snap speed slows.
  Stop: slower speed, lost trunk control or pain.
- Lat Pulldown — 2x6 @ RPE 6, keep reps crisp.
- DB Alternating Incline Press (30-45°) — 2x6 @ RPE 6, keep reps crisp.
  Cue: Brace your trunk; control movement without grinding reps.
  Stop: pain or lost trunk/joint control.
- Single-Leg Forward Hops — 3x4 reps at crisp intent; rest 90s.
  Cue: Stick landings; knees track toes, reset before take-off.
  Stop: pain, poorer jumps or unstable landings.
- Cross-Body Carry — 3 carries over 20 m, heavy but unbroken; rest 90s.
  Cue: Brace; step steadily without twisting or jerking the load.
  Stop: pain or loss of trunk/step control.
- Plank — 2 x 20s hold @ RPE 6; rest 60s.

### D-24 (Monday) — Aerobic support
- Heavy Bag Aerobic Pace — 10min continuous steady pace; RPE 6
  Cue: Low-intensity bag work; rhythm focused
  Stop: dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness.

### D-23 (Tuesday) — Tactical Focus
Why: See the routes that repeatedly move an exchange between striking, clinch, fence and ground.
- Phase Route Review (MMA): 10 minutes, tactical review only. No physical load.
  Step 1: Label the start and end phase of five representative exchanges.
  Step 2: Mark the single cue that caused each transition rather than every action inside it.
  Step 3: Identify the route where the athlete most often arrives out of position.
  Intent: Recognise phase changes early enough to arrive organised.
  Focus: The contact or distance cue that ends one phase and starts the next.
  Reset: If the route becomes unclear, protect posture and return to the last stable position.
  Anchor: Know which phase is arriving.
  Purpose: Finish each transition with stance, grips or base ready for effective offence.
  Progress: Review that route in the next technical session and confirm an organised arrival.

### D-10 (Monday) — Fence Exit to Center Rebase
Why: Defensive MMA cage footwork: leave the fence laterally and recover center-facing stance instead of backing straight into the boundary or turning away from the opponent.
- Fence Exit to Center Rebase: 2 sets x 4 clean exits each direction, full stance reset between reps Rest: 60 sec between sets.
  Cue: Start one step from a marked fence line. On a self-start, light partner pressure, or random left/right cue, circle along the boundary, turn the corner, and rebase facing center before resetting.
  Cue Method: Self-administer a random visual cue (reaction-cue app or a randomized left/right/shot call sequence) so the read stays genuinely reactive without a helper.
  Side / Stance: Start in your orthodox stance and work both directions evenly.
  Quality Stop: Stop the set when the exit becomes a straight retreat, the feet cross, or the athlete finishes with the hips turned away from center.

### D-9 (Tuesday) — Tactical Focus
Why: Prepare the next decision after the matchup's most likely phase transition.
- Second-Phase Decision (MMA): 10 minutes, tactical review only. No physical load.
  Step 1: Select the transition most likely to define the matchup.
  Step 2: Study the expected opponent's first recovery action after that transition.
  Step 3: Assign one continuation and one unfamiliar-reaction reset.
  Intent: Make the second action from the expected opponent's recovery position.
  Focus: Their posture immediately after the planned strike, clinch break, takedown or stand-up.
  Reset: If the recovery differs from the studied picture, stabilise and reassess before continuing.
  Anchor: Read the landing of the transition.
  Purpose: Finish with effective offence from the first stable position in phase two.
  Progress: Rehearse the second decision from the realistic transition, not a static start.
